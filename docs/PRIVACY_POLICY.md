# Privacy Policy — Zed Chat

_Last updated: 2026-10-05 · Contact: `{your-contact-email}` · Publisher: `{your-legal-entity}`_

Zed Chat ("the App") is a mobile application that hosts the publicly available website
chat.z.ai in a native application shell. This policy explains what data the App itself
handles. It must be hosted at a public URL and linked from the store listing.

**Unofficial client notice.** Zed Chat is an independent application. It is not affiliated
with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Z.ai. All AI services, account management, and content
you access through the App are provided by Z.ai and governed by the [Z.ai Terms](https://z.ai)
and [Z.ai Privacy Policy](https://z.ai). Please review those documents for the handling of
your chats, prompts, uploads, and account data.

## 1. Data the App handles

**Website session data (stored on your device only).** To keep you signed in, the App
allows chat.z.ai to store cookies, local storage, and IndexedDB in the App's private
storage on your device. This data never leaves your device except as part of the normal
HTTPS requests between the App and chat.z.ai, exactly as it would in a mobile browser.

**Files you share or download.** Content you share into the App via the Android share
sheet and files you download from chat sessions are temporarily written to the App's
private cache. They are not uploaded anywhere by the App; downloads go to the destination
you choose via the system dialog.

**Notifications.** If a long-running task completes while the App is in the background,
the App may show a local notification. These notifications are generated on your device;
no push service or server is involved.

**Biometric unlock (optional).** If you enable the App Lock feature, authentication is
performed by your device's biometric hardware. Biometric data is handled exclusively by
your device's operating system (e.g., Android Keystore / Apple Secure Enclave). The App
never sees or stores biometric data.

## 2. Data the App does NOT collect

The App does not include advertising SDKs, analytics trackers of its own, location
access, contact access, or any data-broker integration. The App requests only the
permissions strictly required for its features: camera/microphone (for site features like
attaching photos or voice input, always gated by a system prompt), notifications, and
biometrics (only if you enable App Lock).

## 3. Crash reporting (optional, disclosed)

If the publisher enables crash reporting, the App may send crash reports (stack traces,
device model, OS version, app version) to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) to diagnose
crashes. Crash reports do not include your chats or account credentials. This build's
current state is: `{crash reporting enabled / disabled}`. Disable at any time by
contacting the publisher.

## 4. Third parties

- **Z.ai** — all chat/AI functionality, accounts, and content. See their privacy policy.
- **Google/GitHub/Apple** — only if you choose their "sign in with" options on
  chat.z.ai; that flow is provided by Z.ai, not by the App.
- **Sentry** — crash reporting only if enabled above.

## 5. Data retention & deletion

Device-local data (cookies, cache) can be erased with **Settings → Clear browsing data**
or by uninstalling the App. Data held by Z.ai (account, chat history) is governed by
Z.ai's own policies; contact Z.ai to delete your account.

## 6. Children

The App is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the App's store listing and this
page's "last updated" date.
